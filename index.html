Runway

Start-ups run out of cash. Students do too.

Runway turns a lump-sum student loan into a weekly number, lets students check a purchase before they make it, and keeps BNPL, overdrafts and the student loan in one view.

What it does

A weekly figure you can trust
Sets aside rent and regular costs, then spreads what’s left across the weeks until the next loan payment lands.
Ask before you spend
Shows what a purchase does to this week and next, then hands the decision back. No lectures, no verdicts.
Every debt in one place
Shows the real cost of Klarna, an overdraft or a loan before it’s added, and what the student loan means after graduation.
